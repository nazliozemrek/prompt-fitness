        Eco-Fitness

        Your Eco-Fitness combines how you prompt, how long replies run, model choice, carried context and your daily goal.

          How is this calculated?

          Eco-Fitness is a weighted average of five factors, each from 0 to 100: prompt efficiency 35%, output efficiency 20%, model sizing 20%, unnecessary context 10%, footprint vs. your goal 15%.

          The thresholds are rules of thumb that reward the habits which cut the most compute: clear prompts, replies of ~300 tokens or fewer, the smallest model that fits, and fresh chats instead of long history.

          It describes your habits today, not a measured environmental impact.

              Methodology

              Estimates, not measurements.

              AI providers don't publish per-request water or energy data, and nobody outside can know which data center served you. That's why footprints are shown as ranges.

              MeasuredImported API usage
              Token counts you paste from a provider's usage data are the provider's own numbers.
              EstimatedTokens and reply size
              Counted from your text (about 4 characters per token) and from what the prompt asks for. Real tokenizers differ.
              ModeledWater, energy, carbon
              Calculated from those tokens with published per-model assumptions. We show half to double our central estimate; published figures vary even more.
              Why they're still useful
              The absolute numbers are uncertain, but the comparisons hold: a shorter reply, a lighter model or less repeated context uses less, whatever the exact figure.
              Footprint formula 
              weighted = output + 0.2 × input
water (L) = weighted / 10,000 × model factor
energy (kWh) = water / 3.6 L per kWh
CO₂e (g) = energy × 370 g per kWh

              Model factors (0.08–0.11 L light, 0.48–0.52 L standard, 1.45–1.5 L heavy per 10K tokens) follow UC Riverside's "Making AI Less Thirsty", which counts both data-center cooling water and water used to generate the electricity. Differences within a class are illustrative.

              Input tokens are weighted at 0.2 because reading a prompt takes far less compute than generating a reply. That's why controlling reply length usually saves more than shortening your prompt.

              For comparison, Google's August 2025 report puts a median Gemini text prompt at about 0.26 mL of on-site water only, so these estimates sit toward the high end.

              Efficiency score rules

              The score is rule-based and runs on your device; no AI reads your prompt. It weighs conciseness (filler phrases, 25%), focus (repeated phrases or sentences, 20%), clarity (a clear task, 20%), specificity (who it's for, the goal, constraints, 15%) and reply control (a requested length or format, 20%).

              It then subtracts up to 10 points for a bigger model than the task needs and up to 10 for long chat history carried into every message.

              Short isn't automatically better: very short or vague requests are capped (at 35 and 70), because follow-up rounds cost more than a clear first message.

              Tokens are estimated from characters and words (about 4 characters per token in English). Real tokenizers differ by provider. For reasoning models we assume 3 hidden thinking tokens per visible reply token.

              Use Prompt Fitness with your AI

              Every method works on your device. Your prompts and keys are never saved or sent.

              Works in any app

              Share → Prompt Fitness

              1. In ChatGPT, Claude, Notes or any app, select your prompt.
              2. Tap Share, then Prompt Fitness. (First time: tap More and add it to your favorites.)
              3. Tap Copy improved prompt and paste it back into your chat.

              The text is checked on your phone and never saved.

              Or chat inside Prompt Fitness

              Open your AI here and sign in as usual. Each reply's footprint shows in a panel at the bottom as it finishes.

              Measured on this device; only numbers are kept. Google doesn't allow sign-in inside apps, so for Gemini or "Continue with Google" use the Safari extension below.

              Use it with ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini

                Prompt Fitness isn't connected to any AI service: you stay in control of what you send. A browser extension that does this automatically is coming soon.

                Automatic tracking in your browser

                  Settings› Apps› Safari› Extensions› Prompt Fitness

                  What it never does

                  Store or send your messages, read other websites, or run without a visible indicator. You can pause it per site at any time from its popup.

                  Share to score

                  1. In any AI app or browser, select your prompt text.
                  2. Tap Share and choose Prompt Fitness.
                  3. The prompt opens in the playground with its score and tips. Nothing is saved until you tap Log prompt, and even then only the numbers are kept.

                  Mobile Chrome doesn't support extensions, so automatic tracking isn't available on Android.

                  Zero-storage API usage tracking

                  Why there's no API key field

                  Usage APIs from AI providers require an organization admin key, which can manage your whole account. Don't paste admin keys into any app, including this one.

                  Safer ways to add real usage

                  1. Log the usage object your own code already receives. Your key stays in your app; only token counts come here.
                  2. Paste a single usage JSON below. It's parsed on this device and nothing is sent anywhere.
