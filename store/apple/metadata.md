## Description (4000)
```
Prompt Fitness helps you use AI assistants more efficiently, with less water and energy.

Every AI answer runs on data-center hardware that needs electricity and cooling. Prompt Fitness estimates the water, energy and carbon behind each prompt and coaches you toward prompts that get the same quality with less waste.

SCORE YOUR PROMPTS
• A Prompt Efficiency Score from 0 to 100, updated as you type
• Checks for filler phrases, repetition, unclear tasks and missing length or format instructions
• Warns when a heavy reasoning model is used for a simple question

TIPS THAT SHOW THEIR IMPACT
• Every tip shows how much water it would save per prompt and per month
• Learn why reply length matters more than prompt length

TRACK YOUR DAY
• Set a daily water budget and watch your Eco-Fitness score
• See the last 7 days and compare models side by side
• Everyday equivalents: bottles of water, phone charges, toilet flushes

SAFARI EXTENSION
• Turn on the included Safari extension to measure your chats on ChatGPT, Claude and Gemini automatically
• It reads message length on your device and keeps only numbers. Your conversations are never stored or sent anywhere

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking
• Everything runs and stays on your device. The app makes no network requests
• Delete all data at any time

ABOUT THE NUMBERS
The figures are research-based estimates, not measurements. AI providers don't publish per-prompt water or energy data, and the real footprint depends on the data center that answers you. The app explains its method and sources in the "How the numbers work" section.

Prompt Fitness is not affiliated with OpenAI, Anthropic or Google.
```
