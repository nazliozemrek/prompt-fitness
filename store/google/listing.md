## Full description (4000)
```
Prompt Fitness helps you use AI assistants more efficiently, with less water and energy.

Every AI answer runs on data-center hardware that needs electricity and cooling. Prompt Fitness estimates the water, energy and carbon behind each prompt and coaches you toward prompts that get the same quality with less waste.

SCORE YOUR PROMPTS
• A Prompt Efficiency Score from 0 to 100, updated as you type
• Checks for filler, repetition, unclear tasks and missing length or format instructions
• Warns when a heavy reasoning model is used for a simple question

SHARE TO SCORE
• Select a prompt in any app, tap Share, and choose Prompt Fitness to score it instantly

TIPS THAT SHOW THEIR IMPACT
• Every tip shows how much water it would save per prompt and per month

TRACK YOUR DAY
• Daily water budget, Eco-Fitness score, 7-day history and model comparison
• Everyday equivalents: bottles of water, phone charges, toilet flushes

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking
• The app has no internet permission. Everything stays on your device
• Delete all data at any time

ABOUT THE NUMBERS
The figures are research-based estimates, not measurements. The app explains its method and sources.

Not affiliated with OpenAI, Anthropic or Google.
```
