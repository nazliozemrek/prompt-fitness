# Prompt Fitness Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** [DATE]  
**Applies to:** the Prompt Fitness app for iOS, iPadOS, macOS and Android, the Prompt Fitness Safari extension, and the Prompt Fitness Chrome extension.

## Summary
Prompt Fitness does not collect, transmit, sell or share any personal data. Everything the app and extensions do happens on your device.

## What is processed on your device
- **Prompt text you type or share into the app, and message text on supported AI chat websites** (chatgpt.com, claude.ai, gemini.google.com) when the browser extension is turned on. This text is read in your device's memory only to count its approximate length and score it. It is never saved and never sent anywhere.
- **Usage numbers:** the time of each prompt, the selected model, estimated token counts, and the efficiency score. These are stored on your device so you can see your daily and weekly totals. Records older than 30 days are deleted automatically.
- **Settings:** your daily water budget, sites where tracking is paused, and the model you chose for each site.

## What is not collected
We do not collect names, email addresses, identifiers, location, contacts, browsing history, or the content of your conversations. The app and extensions contain no analytics, advertising, crash-reporting or tracking software, and they make no network requests.

## Sharing between the extension and the app (iOS)
On iPhone and iPad, the Safari extension passes usage numbers to the Prompt Fitness app through a private storage area on your device (an App Group). This data never leaves the device.

## Backups
On Android, app data is excluded from cloud backup and device transfer. On Apple devices, app data may be included in your own encrypted iCloud or computer backups, which are controlled by your Apple account, not by us.

## Your choices
- Delete all data at any time: "Clear local data" in the app, or "Clear tracked data" in the extension popup.
- Pause tracking for any site from the extension popup, or turn the extension off in your browser settings.
- Uninstalling the app or extension removes its data from your device.

## Children
Prompt Fitness is not directed to children under 13 and collects no data from anyone.

## Changes
If this policy changes, we will update the effective date above and describe the change in the app's release notes.
