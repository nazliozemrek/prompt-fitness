      How the numbers work

      Estimates, not measurements.

      AI providers don't publish per-prompt water or energy data, and we can't know which data center served you. Real values can be several times lower or higher.

      Footprint formula 
      weighted = output + 0.2 × input
water (L) = weighted / 10,000 × model factor
energy (kWh) = water / 3.6 L per kWh
CO₂e (g) = energy × 370 g per kWh

      Model factors (0.08–0.11 L light, 0.48–0.52 L standard, 1.45–1.5 L heavy per 10K tokens) follow UC Riverside's "Making AI Less Thirsty", which counts both data-center cooling water and water used to generate the electricity. Differences within a class are illustrative.

      Input tokens are weighted at 0.2 because reading a prompt takes far less compute than generating a reply. That's why controlling reply length usually saves more than shortening your prompt.

      For comparison, Google's August 2025 report puts a median Gemini text prompt at about 0.26 mL of on-site water only, so these estimates sit toward the high end.

      Efficiency score rules

      The score is rule-based and runs on your device; no AI reads your prompt. It weighs conciseness (filler phrases, 30%), focus (repeated phrases or sentences, 25%), clarity (a clear task and enough context, 25%) and reply control (a requested length or format, 20%).

      It then subtracts up to 10 points for a heavy model on a simple request and up to 10 for long chat history carried into every message.

      Short isn't automatically better: a vague two-word prompt scores low, because follow-up rounds cost more than a clear first message.

      Tokens are estimated from characters and words (about 4 characters per token in English). Real tokenizers differ by provider. For reasoning models we assume 3 hidden thinking tokens per visible reply token.

      Automatic tracking in your browser

        Settings› Apps› Safari› Extensions› Prompt Fitness

        What it never does

        Store or send your messages, read other websites, or run without a visible indicator. You can pause it per site at any time from its popup.

        Share to score

        1. In any AI app or browser, select your prompt text.
        2. Tap Share and choose Prompt Fitness.
        3. The prompt opens in the playground with its score and tips. Nothing is saved until you tap Log prompt, and even then only the numbers are kept.

        Mobile Chrome doesn't support extensions, so automatic tracking isn't available on Android.

        Zero-storage API usage tracking

        Why there's no API key field

        Usage APIs from AI providers require an organization admin key, which can manage your whole account. Don't paste admin keys into any app, including this one.

        Safer ways to add real usage

        1. Log the usage object your own code already receives. Your key stays in your app; only token counts come here.
        2. Paste a single usage JSON below. It's parsed on this device and nothing is sent anywhere.
